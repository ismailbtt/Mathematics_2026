
# Exercise 1. Matrix Size and Entries

## Question

Given

$$
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1 & 3\\
0 & 4 & 5
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B=
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0\\
-2 & 3\\
4 & 1
\end{pmatrix}
$$

1. State the sizes of matrices A and B.
2. Find the entries $a_{12}$, $a_{23}$, $b_{21}$, and $b_{32}$.
3. Write the second row of A and the first column of B as vectors.

---

## Solution 1. Matrix Sizes

Matrix A has 2 rows and 3 columns.

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{A: 2\times3}
$$

Matrix B has 3 rows and 2 columns.

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{B: 3\times2}
$$

**Explanation:** We write matrix dimensions as the number of rows followed by the number of columns.

## Solution 2. Matrix Entries

The first index indicates the row, and the second index indicates the column.

For matrix A:

$$
a_{12}=-1
$$

Because -1 is in row 1, column 2.

$$
a_{23}=5
$$

Because 5 is in row 2, column 3.

For matrix B:

$$
b_{21}=-2
$$

Because -2 is in row 2, column 1.

$$
b_{32}=1
$$

Because 1 is in row 3, column 2.

**Final Answer:**

$$
\boxed{
a_{12}=-1,\quad
a_{23}=5,\quad
b_{21}=-2,\quad
b_{32}=1
}
$$

## Solution 3. Row and Column Vectors

The second row of A is:

$$
\boxed{\begin{pmatrix}0&4&5\end{pmatrix}}
$$

The first column of B is:

$$
\boxed{\begin{pmatrix}1\\-2\\4\end{pmatrix}}
$$

**Explanation:** A row is horizontal, while a column is vertical.

## Verification

All entries and vectors correspond to their positions in the original matrices.
