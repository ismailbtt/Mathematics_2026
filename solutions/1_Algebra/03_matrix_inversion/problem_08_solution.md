
The original transformation is:

$$
S\begin{pmatrix}
x\\
y
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
2x\\
\frac{y}{2}
\end{pmatrix}
$$

This means:

- The x-coordinate is stretched by a factor of 2.
- The y-coordinate is compressed by a factor of 1/2.

The inverse transformation reverses these effects:

$$
S^{-1}\begin{pmatrix}
x\\
y
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
\frac{x}{2}\\
2y
\end{pmatrix}
$$
