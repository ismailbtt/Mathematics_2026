
# Exercise 7 – A Line Relative to a Plane

## Question

For:

L: (x, y, z) = (1, 1, 0) + t(1, -1, 0)

Π: x + y + z = 2

Determine whether the line intersects the plane at one point, is parallel to it, or lies in it.

Explain the role of the dot product of the direction vector and the normal vector.

## Solution

### Step 1: Identify the Direction and Normal Vectors

The direction vector of the line is:

v = (1, -1, 0)

The normal vector of the plane is:

n = (1, 1, 1)

### Step 2: Calculate the Dot Product

Calculate:

v · n = (1)(1) + (-1)(1) + (0)(1)

v · n = 1 - 1 + 0

v · n = 0

Since the dot product is zero, the direction vector is perpendicular to the normal vector.

Therefore, the line is parallel to the plane or lies entirely in it.

### Step 3: Check Whether the Line Lies in the Plane

The line passes through:

P = (1, 1, 0)

Substitute this point into the plane equation:

x + y + z = 2

1 + 1 + 0 = 2

2 = 2

The point lies in the plane.

Since the direction vector is parallel to the plane and one point of the line lies in the plane, the entire line lies in the plane.

## Final Answer

The line lies entirely in the plane.

**Direction vector:**

v = (1, -1, 0)

**Normal vector:**

n = (1, 1, 1)

**Dot product:**

v · n = 0

The line is contained in the plane because its direction vector is perpendicular to the normal vector and its given point belongs to the plane.
