
# Exercise 4 – Dot Product and Perpendicularity

Given:

u = (1, 2, -1)

v = (2, -1, 3)

## 1. Calculate the Dot Product

The dot product is calculated by multiplying corresponding components and adding the results.

Formula:

u · v = x1*x2 + y1*y2 + z1*z2

Substitute the coordinates:

u · v = (1*2) + (2*(-1)) + (-1*3)

u · v = 2 - 2 - 3

**Result:**

u · v = -3

## 2. Determine Whether the Vectors Are Perpendicular

Two vectors are perpendicular if their dot product is equal to zero.

Condition:

u · v = 0

However:

u · v = -3

Since -3 is not equal to 0, the vectors are not perpendicular.

**Result:**

The vectors u and v are NOT perpendicular.

## 3. Find a Such That w = (2, -1, a) Is Perpendicular to u

Given:

w = (2, -1, a)

For perpendicular vectors, the dot product must be zero.

Condition:

u · w = 0

Substitute the coordinates:

(1, 2, -1) · (2, -1, a) = 0

Calculate:

1*2 + 2*(-1) + (-1)*a = 0

2 - 2 - a = 0

-a = 0

**Result:**

a = 0

Therefore:

w = (2, -1, 0)

## 4. Verify the Result

Calculate the dot product:

u · w = (1*2) + (2*(-1)) + (-1*0)

u · w = 2 - 2 + 0

u · w = 0

Since the dot product is zero, the vectors are perpendicular.

**Verification:**

u · w = 0

## Final Answers

- u · v = -3
- Vectors u and v are not perpendicular.
- a = 0
- w = (2, -1, 0)
- Vectors u and w are perpendicular.
