
# Exercise 4 – Dot Product and Perpendicularity

## Question

For u = (1, 2, -1) and v = (2, -1, 3), compute u · v and determine whether the vectors are perpendicular.

Then find a so that w = (2, -1, a) is perpendicular to u, and check the result.

---

## Solution

### 1. Calculate the Dot Product

The dot product formula is:

u · v = x1*x2 + y1*y2 + z1*z2

Substitute:

u · v = (1*2) + (2*(-1)) + (-1*3)

= 2 - 2 - 3

**Result:**

u · v = -3

### 2. Determine Whether the Vectors Are Perpendicular

Two vectors are perpendicular if their dot product equals zero.

Condition:

u · v = 0

However:

u · v = -3

Since -3 is not equal to zero, the vectors are not perpendicular.

**Result:**

u and v are NOT perpendicular.

### 3. Find a Such That w Is Perpendicular to u

Given:

w = (2, -1, a)

For perpendicular vectors:

u · w = 0

Substitute:

(1, 2, -1) · (2, -1, a) = 0

1*2 + 2*(-1) + (-1)*a = 0

2 - 2 - a = 0

-a = 0

**Result:**

a = 0

Therefore:

w = (2, -1, 0)

### 4. Verify the Result

Calculate the dot product:

u · w = (1*2) + (2*(-1)) + (-1*0)

= 2 - 2 + 0

= 0

Since the dot product is zero, the vectors are perpendicular.

---

## Final Answers

- u · v = -3
- u and v are not perpendicular.
- a = 0
- w = (2, -1, 0)
- u · w = 0
