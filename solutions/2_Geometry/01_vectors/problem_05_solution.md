
# Exercise 5 – Angle Between Vectors

## Question

Given u = (1, 1, 0) and v = (1, 0, 1):

1. Use the sign of u · v to predict the type of angle.
2. Compute the angle.
3. Compare the result with the prediction.

---

## Solution

### 1. Predict the Type of Angle Using the Dot Product

The dot product formula is:

u · v = x1*x2 + y1*y2 + z1*z2

Substitute:

u · v = (1*1) + (1*0) + (0*1)

= 1 + 0 + 0

**Result:**

u · v = 1

Since the dot product is positive, the angle is acute (less than 90 degrees).

### 2. Calculate the Angle Between the Vectors

The angle formula is:

cos(theta) = (u · v) / (||u|| * ||v||)

First, calculate the length of u:

||u|| = sqrt(1² + 1² + 0²)

= sqrt(2)

Now calculate the length of v:

||v|| = sqrt(1² + 0² + 1²)

= sqrt(2)

Substitute:

cos(theta) = 1 / (sqrt(2) * sqrt(2))

= 1/2

Take the inverse cosine:

theta = arccos(1/2)

**Result:**

theta = 60 degrees

### 3. Compare the Result with the Prediction

The dot product was positive:

u · v = 1

Therefore, we predicted an acute angle.

The calculated angle is 60 degrees.

Since 60 degrees is less than 90 degrees, the prediction was correct.

---

## Final Answers

- Dot product: u · v = 1
- ||u|| = sqrt(2)
- ||v|| = sqrt(2)
- cos(theta) = 1/2
- Angle: theta = 60 degrees
- The angle is acute.
