
# Exercise 5 – Angle Between Vectors

Given:

u = (1, 1, 0)

v = (1, 0, 1)

## 1. Predict the Type of Angle Using the Dot Product

The dot product formula is:

u · v = x1*x2 + y1*y2 + z1*z2

Substitute the coordinates:

u · v = (1*1) + (1*0) + (0*1)

u · v = 1 + 0 + 0

**Result:**

u · v = 1

Since the dot product is positive, the angle between the vectors is acute (less than 90 degrees).

## 2. Calculate the Angle Between the Vectors

The angle formula is:

cos(theta) = (u · v) / (||u|| * ||v||)

First, calculate the length of each vector.

Length of u:

||u|| = √(1² + 1² + 0²)

||u|| = √2

Length of v:

||v|| = √(1² + 0² + 1²)

||v|| = √2

Now substitute into the angle formula:

cos(theta) = 1 / (√2 * √2)

cos(theta) = 1 / 2

To find the angle, take the inverse cosine:

theta = arccos(1/2)

**Result:**

theta = 60 degrees

## 3. Compare the Result with the Prediction

The dot product was positive:

u · v = 1

Therefore, we predicted that the angle would be acute.

The calculated angle is:

theta = 60 degrees

Since 60 degrees is less than 90 degrees, the prediction was correct.

## Final Answers

- Dot product: u · v = 1
- Length of u: ||u|| = √2
- Length of v: ||v|| = √2
- Cosine of the angle: cos(theta) = 1/2
- Angle: theta = 60 degrees
- The angle is acute.
